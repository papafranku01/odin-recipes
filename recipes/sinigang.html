Sinigang Recipe


Sinigang is a popular Filipino soup or stew defined by its signature savory and sour taste, usually achieved using tamarind, unripe mangoes, guava, or other sour fruits. It is a versatile comfort food typically cooked with pork, fish, shrimp, or beef, along with vegetables like spinach, okra, radish, and eggplant.

The Ingredients:

  • 250g Pork Belly
  • Pork broth cubes
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Okra
  • Radish
  • Kangkong (Water Spinach)
  • Green Chili

How to Cook Sinigang:

  1. Cook the pork belly until it browns, then remove from the pan
  2. Prepare the veggies
  3. Saute onion and garlic in the pot
  4. Add water and broth cube, and put back the cooked pork belly
  5. Add vegetables and cook until softened
  6. Enjoy!
